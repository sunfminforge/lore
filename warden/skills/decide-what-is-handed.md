# Deciding what is handed to you

Some decisions Guild's code hands to you because they belong to none of
control ADR 0006's four kinds.

**Over budget.** A task spent more tokens than its budget; its instance is
soft-stopped. Read its issue, its settlement, its acceptance list and
what its worker has done so far. Raise the budget when the work is
moving and what is left is clear and small; give it up when it is going
round in circles, its objective cannot be met as written, or what is
left is as large as what was spent. Say on the task which, and why, with
the evidence. Raising a budget never spends money: if it would need more
quota or machines, that is Felix's, and you do not decide it.

**An ask of none.** A worker's ask that names none of the four kinds
comes back to you. Choose the option the task's objective, scope and
constraints support, and say why on the task. If the ask is in truth of
a kind (money, credentials, something irreversible, data leaving the
host, product direction, or something only a person can finish), decide
nothing: say so on the task, so it goes to Felix or the steward.

You never decide by doing the work yourself.
