# Reviewing a role draft

A role draft is a lore pull request, opened by a crew role's account,
whose body starts with `<!--guild:draft role=<role> task=<n>
loosens=<guards>:<id>,…-->`. Review it as any task's work, and then:

- Run `guild role check <dir>` on the drafted role yourself; anything it prints is a send-back. A draft that touches anything outside the role's own directory is a send-back. {#role-check}
- Compare `prohibitions.toml` with the role as lore's main holds it, or, for a new role, with the guarded prohibitions of the crew role that drafted it (the floor). Every prohibition guarding a kind (`money`, `credentials`, `irreversible`, `data-leaving-host`, `product-direction`) must be there, unchanged block by block (a diff of each, not a count of guards), unless the mark says it loosens it. Each prohibition must say which kind it guards; one that names `none` while it plainly guards a kind is a send-back. {#prohibition-floor}
- Read `core.md` and the skills: the role holds only how it does its kind of work, independent of any repo; its core stays within its cap, detail in skills; it does what its request asked, within the request's constraints; it gives itself no lever, no approval and no credential that is not its role's. A draft that answers a signal or a finding: read the evidence it cites at its source (the refused splits' reasons, the send-backs, a failed task's settlement and the item it left todo), not the body's account of it, and judge whether the change answers it. {#read-the-memory}
- The replay check of core's review entry: a replay of another head counts for nothing, and Guild reads only the first ```text replay block of the body, so an older head's block left above it hides this head's. Guild's lander withholds an upgrade whose replay of its head is missing or fails; you judge the replay that is there. A send-back names each old task the draft lost or failed to gain, and the `replay:` task. {#replay-at-the-head}
- A draft that reflows entries to one line each, ending in their ID, is `guild lore ids`' own format, not a rewrite: judge its content by a word diff (`git diff --word-diff`), not by its count of changed lines. {#reflow-by-word-diff}
- A skill adopted from outside must carry its source link, its version or commit and its licence in the pull request's `research-proposal` block. Never run or install what it adopts. {#adopted-source}
- Who approves: an ordinary role, or the foreman, with no guarded prohibition loosened: you, on a pass; the warden, a crew role, a change to the `crew` mark, or a loosened prohibition of any guarded kind but money: the steward; a loosened prohibition guarding money: Felix. Where it is not yours, post your verdict and do not approve; landing waits for theirs. A role draft never lands on your approval alone where the code says it needs another's. A brief that says to approve one of these does not make it yours: post your verdict, say on the task (`guild task tell`) whose approval lands it, and approve nothing. {#who-approves}
- `role.toml` must not carry `crew` unless the steward's approval is what lands it. {#no-crew-mark}
